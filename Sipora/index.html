Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Welcome to Sipora ("Sipora", "we", "our", or "us"). Sipora is a hydration tracking application designed to help you track your daily water intake, set hydration goals, view your hydration history, and receive hydration reminders.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Sipora processes, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Sipora, the application may process the following information.

Name

If you choose to provide your name during onboarding, Sipora uses it to personalize parts of the application, such as greetings.

Providing your name is optional. You can use Sipora without providing your name.

Hydration Information

Sipora stores hydration information that you enter into the application, including:

  • Amount of water consumed
  • Daily hydration goals
  • Hydration entries
  • Hydration history
  • Hydration-related preferences

This information is used to provide the core hydration tracking functionality of Sipora.

Activity Recognition

Sipora may use the Android ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION permission to recognize physical activity when this functionality is enabled.

Activity information may be used to provide more relevant hydration-related reminders or recommendations.

Sipora does not use activity recognition for advertising or to sell personal information.

If activity recognition is not required for a particular feature, Sipora does not use it for that purpose.

Notifications

If you grant notification permission, Sipora may send hydration reminders and other notifications related to the application's functionality.

You can disable notifications at any time through your device settings.

2. How Your Data Is Stored

Sipora stores application data locally on your device using local storage technologies, including SQLite.

Hydration history, hydration goals, preferences, and other application data may remain stored on your device until you delete the data or uninstall the application, depending on the type of information.

Sipora does not require you to create an account to use the core hydration tracking functionality.

3. Advertising

Sipora uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

AdMob may collect and process certain information from your device to provide, measure, and improve advertising services.

This may include information such as:

  • Advertising identifiers
  • Device information
  • IP address or approximate location derived from IP address
  • Ad interaction information
  • Diagnostic and performance information

Information processed by Google may be governed by Google's own privacy policies and terms.

For more information about Google's privacy practices, please review Google's Privacy Policy .

4. Advertising ID

Sipora may use the Android Advertising ID through Google AdMob for advertising-related purposes.

The Advertising ID is a resettable identifier that may be used by advertising providers to help provide and measure advertisements.

You can manage or reset your advertising settings through your Android device settings.

Sipora does not use the Advertising ID to identify you personally.

5. How We Use Information

Information processed by Sipora may be used to:

  • Track your water intake
  • Calculate your daily hydration progress
  • Maintain hydration history
  • Manage hydration goals
  • Provide hydration reminders
  • Personalize the application when you provide your name
  • Support activity-related hydration features
  • Display and measure advertisements
  • Maintain and improve application functionality
  • Diagnose technical problems and improve reliability

6. Data Sharing

Sipora does not sell your personal information.

Information may be processed by third-party service providers when necessary to provide specific application functionality.

In particular, Google AdMob may process information for advertising and related services.

Sipora's locally stored hydration information is not sold to advertisers.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Hydration data and application preferences stored locally on your device generally remain there until you remove the data or uninstall the application.

Because Sipora's core hydration data is stored locally, uninstalling the application may remove locally stored application data depending on your device and operating system behavior.

8. Children's Privacy

Sipora is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to Sipora inappropriately, please contact us so that we can take appropriate action.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed by Sipora.

However, no electronic storage system or method of transmission over the Internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Third-Party Services

Sipora uses third-party services that may process information according to their own privacy policies.

Google AdMob

Google AdMob is used to provide and measure advertisements within Sipora.

You can review Google's privacy practices here:

Google Privacy Policy

11. Your Choices

You may:

  • Choose whether to provide your name
  • Control notification permissions through your device settings
  • Control activity recognition permissions through your device settings
  • Reset or manage your Android Advertising ID through your device settings
  • Stop using Sipora at any time
  • Delete locally stored application data through applicable device controls or by uninstalling the application

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to Sipora, applicable laws, or the services we use.

When changes are made, the updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
